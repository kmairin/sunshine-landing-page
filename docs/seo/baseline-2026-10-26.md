## The big problem found

Every page told Google that its "real" address (the canonical link) was
`https://www.sunshinecoinlaundry.com/...`. That domain is a parked GoDaddy page, not this site.
robots.txt and the sitemap pointed there too, so Google was being told to ignore this site in
favour of an empty page. The live site is `https://cclaundrysystem.com` (Cloudflare DNS in front of
GitHub Pages; www and http both 301 to it correctly).

## Where the shop actually is

- US Census geocoder and the Google Maps pin (33.9378257, -118.2912203) put the shop inside the
  City of Los Angeles, census tract 2412.02, on the east side of Vermont Ave between 108th and
  109th St. OpenStreetMap calls that area Vermont Vista.
- Westmont (an unincorporated part of LA County) starts on the west side of Vermont, across the
  street. West Athens is to the south-west, Vermont Knolls to the north.
- The site said "Vermont-Slauson", which is about 3 miles north. Fixed.
